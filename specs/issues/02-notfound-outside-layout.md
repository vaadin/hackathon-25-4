Withdrawn. The built in not found view renders outside the `@Layout` automatic layout, and that is documented: "Error views are not routes, so automatic layouts do not apply to them. To show an error view inside your main layout, add `@ParentLayout` to it", in `building-apps/ui-basics/handle-errors`. Our subclass with `@ParentLayout` is the documented way, not a workaround. The reproducer confirmed the behaviour and was deleted.
